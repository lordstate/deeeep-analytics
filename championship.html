The Deeeep.io Championship.

A brand new, year-round format running alongside the existing Elo — independent from past tournaments, with its own season points and its own path to a 16-player World Championship. This page is a live beta: the rules below are final, the season hasn't started yet.

How a season works

  • 1 season = 1 calendar year, made of 12 Monthly Championships — one per month.
  • Completely separate from the archived 2023/2024/2026 tournaments. Elo keeps existing on its own, untouched by this system.
  • Championship Points are season-specific and reset to zero every year.
  • 5 regions: EU · NA · AS · OCE · SA. Each player picks one region for the whole season.

Every month

  • Registration opens, open to anyone — no fixed player count. 14, 34, or 267 sign-ups all work the same way.
  • Once registration closes, the site randomly pairs every player with 4 different opponents, known from day one.
  • Maximum 1 match per week, so the 4 matches spread across the month.

A month not played earns 0 points. Ties within a Monthly don't matter — several players can land on the same record and points. What matters is the cumulative annual total per region.

World Championship qualification

16 players qualify for the season-ending World Championship, purely by cumulative Championship Points:

  • Top 6 — EU
  • Top 4 — NA
  • Top 2 — AS
  • Top 2 — OCE
  • Top 2 — SA

The exact finals format is still being finalized. A cash prize is under discussion, most likely concentrated on the World Championship rather than spread thin across regions every month.

⚠️ About this beta tool

Everything below (registrations, matchmaking, results, standings) runs entirely in your browser via local storage — there's no account system and no shared server, consistent with the rest of this site. It's built as a working commissioner tool for testing the format before the season goes live. Data stays on this device only.
