Coming next season

The Deeeep.io Championship.

A brand new, year-round format running alongside the existing Elo — independent from past tournaments, with its own season points and its own path to a 16-player World Championship. This page explains how it will work; the season hasn't started yet, and registration will happen on our Discord, not on this site.

💰 Cash prize

There's over $100 in cash prizes up for grabs, concentrated on the World Championship at the end of the season rather than split into small amounts every month. Exact details will be confirmed closer to launch — but the headline number is real.

How a season works

  • 1 season = 1 calendar year, made of 11 Monthly Championships — one per month. The 12th month is reserved entirely for the World Championship Final.
  • Completely separate from the archived 2023/2024/2026 tournaments. Elo keeps existing on its own, untouched by this system.
  • Championship Points are season-specific and reset to zero every year.
  • 5 regions: EU · NA · AS · OCE · SA. Each player picks one region for the whole season.

Every month — and it's genuinely quick

  • Sign-ups happen on our Discord — open to anyone, no fixed player count. 14, 34, or 267 sign-ups all work the same way.
  • You choose which months you take part in. Nothing is mandatory. Know you're busy in March? Just skip it — you'll simply score 0 points that month, no penalty beyond that.
  • If you do join a given month, it's just 4 matches against 4 different opponents, known from day one — nothing more. That's it.
  • Maximum 1 match per week, so those 4 matches spread naturally across the month. This is designed on purpose to stay low-commitment: a few games spread out, not a demanding grind.

A month not played earns 0 points — there's no downside to sitting one out beyond missing that month's points. Ties within a Monthly don't matter — several players can land on the same record and points. What matters is the cumulative annual total per region.

World Championship qualification

16 players qualify for the season-ending World Championship, purely by cumulative Championship Points:

  • Top 6 — EU
  • Top 4 — NA
  • Top 2 — AS
  • Top 2 — OCE
  • Top 2 — SA

The exact finals format is still being finalized.
